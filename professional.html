Solar & stellar astrophysics

Research

I study the Sun through its oscillations and atmospheric waves, while continuing a research thread in young-star accretion that began during my undergraduate work.

01 · Solar atmosphere

Atmospheric gravity waves

My graduate research includes atmospheric-wave diagnostics in the lower solar atmosphere. I co-authored Atmospheric Gravity Waves Modulated by the Magnetic Field Configuration, published in The Astrophysical Journal in 2025.

02 · Stellar research · 2021–2022

Accretion in young stars

With Prof. Katherine B. Follette and Dr. Connor Robinson, I worked on accreting protoplanets and disk-host star variability in the Giant Accreting Protoplanet Survey. My senior thesis examined second-to-minute Hα variability in transitional disk host-stars, and I continued that analysis as a post-baccalaureate researcher at Amherst College.
